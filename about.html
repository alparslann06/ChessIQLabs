About

Built by players who kept losing the same way.

ChessIQLabs started as a private script: one developer, one painful rating plateau, and a spreadsheet of every blunder from a hundred games. The spreadsheet worked. The platform is the spreadsheet, shipped.

Principles

What we refuse to do.

🔓

No paywalled essentials

Game review and puzzles decide whether this site is useful. Locking them would make the whole product a teaser. They stay free — permanently.

🧠

Honest numbers

Accuracy scores and grades are computed, not inflated. A blunder is called a blunder, even in the report you were proud of.

🚫

No engagement traps

No artificial streak pressure, no dark patterns, no "daily limit" upsells. The habit you build should belong to you, not to our retention curve.

The origin

One spreadsheet, 100 games, +140 Elo.

Every loss got logged: which move, what pattern, what the engine said instead. After a hundred games, three patterns explained nearly every defeat.

Training those three patterns — nothing else, no new openings, no gimmicks — moved the rating faster than a year of unfocused play before it. ChessIQLabs automates exactly that loop for everyone.

  • Review → find the pattern → drill the fix
  • Repeat weekly, measure monthly
  • Trust the boring work
